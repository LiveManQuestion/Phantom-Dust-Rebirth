# Phantom Dust Rebirth: changes from the original game

As of Experimental 98. *(untested)* = waiting for a test in a real match.

## Online
- Epic Online Services instead of Xbox Live: no accounts, no port forwarding
- Faster joins, joins with a wrong PC clock
- No freeze on a short connection drop *(untested)*
- Empty lobbies hidden; lobby join / leave crashes fixed
- Store and unpacked copies, plus Linux (Wine)
- Your own player name, kept after reinstalls
- Local saves

## Lobbies
- Rated / Unrated lobbies, any rule can be Rated
- No rating join limit
- Host's "nametags on bodies" setting applies to all

## Voice
- Voice chat through Epic
- Tag Team: team-only voice in matches
- Mind Reading hears the other team *(untested)*
- Mic or Classic voice mode

## Nametags
- Always on
- Custom colours for allies, enemies, defeated
- Optional nametags on defeated players
- Play Dead shows as defeated
- Players who leave lose their nametag

## Scoreboards
- Global boards back: Rating, Battle Royale, Tag Team, One-on-One wins
- Ghost battles: fight a player's arsenal, AI-driven *(untested)*

## Arsenal Lab
- Buy and sell arsenal cases, also from the lobby
- Test Arsenal: pick both arsenals, fight the AI
- Max credits and skills in the shop

## Menus and UI
- "Rebirth" title artwork
- Build version on the main menu
- Community (Discord) button replaces Help & Support
- "Xbox Live" reads "Online"
- Options: Send Log, Updates, Aura Particle Beeps
- Multiplayer: Settings menu, Online Store removed, Trade reads "Theater"
- Scoreboard: Global boards only
- Lobby: Arsenal Lab button, Rated / Unrated shown
- Lab: Buy, Sell and Test Arsenal rows
- Opponent-feedback screen removed
- Megaphone icon for who's talking

## Controls
- PlayStation, Nintendo and other controllers, with rumble
- Lock onto partner: RB / Tab (key configurable) *(untested)*

## Other
- Quieter start-up logos, game volume remembered
- PD Helper modpacks stay loaded *(untested)*
- Discord lobby announcements
- One-click Setup, Remove, Collect-Log, Edit-Settings
- Auto-updater: Stable, Experimental or Off
- No pausing when minimized
